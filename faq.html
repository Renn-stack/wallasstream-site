Safety & privacy

What it does, and what it never touches

Does Wallas’ Stream record audio?

No. Wallas’ Stream streams audio live only during active use. It does not record, store, or upload audio.

Is Wallas’ Stream a medical device?

No. Wallas’ Stream is not a medical device. It is a personal audio streaming tool, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

Will this affect my hearing device settings?

No. Wallas’ Stream does not change your processor settings or clinical programming in any way. It simply streams audio from your computer to your iOS device, which then delivers sound through your existing hearing setup.

  • No changes to MAPs or audiology settings
  • No firmware updates
  • No medical adjustments
  • Works only as an external audio streaming companion

Compatibility

Devices and platforms

Does Wallas’ Stream support device-connected sound processors?

Yes. Wallas’ Stream has been tested with Cochlear™ Nucleus™ 7 and Nucleus™ 8 processors connected through an iOS device. Compatibility may vary depending on your processor model and device audio settings.

Which processors are supported?

For now, Wallas’ Stream has been tested with Cochlear™ Nucleus™ 7 and Nucleus™ 8 processors. We expect broader compatibility as more users test different brands and models.

Does this work with hearing aids too?

Yes. Wallas’ Stream has also been tested with Rexton hearing aids connected through an iOS device. Compatibility may vary depending on your device and audio setup.

Is Windows support available?

Wallas’ Stream is currently available for computers only. We’re actively working on expanding support to other platforms.

Does this work with Android phones?

Android support may be explored in the future.

Setup & everyday use

Getting it running, and living with it

Do I need to install anything on my computer?

Yes. You need the Wallas’ Stream app for Mac and Wallas Audio, a lightweight virtual audio driver used for streaming. Once installed, you select Wallas Audio as your computer’s input and output device, so your Mac’s sound is routed into Wallas’ Stream.

The download page walks through both installs in order.

Is there audio delay?

Wallas’ Stream is designed for low-latency listening. In most cases, any delay is minimal (typically under 1 second).

Does Wallas’ Stream work in the background?

Yes. Streaming continues while the app runs in the background, so you can keep listening during meetings or classes.

Where can I get support?

You can reach support anytime through our contact page.
