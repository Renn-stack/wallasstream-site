Troubleshooting

When the audio isn’t behaving

Audio is lagging or stuttering

Wi-Fi mode

  1. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network

    If your computer and iOS device are on different networks, audio may lag or disconnect.

  2. For best performance, use 5 GHz Wi-Fi

    A faster Wi-Fi connection reduces latency and improves stability.

USB mode

  1. Confirm the USB cable is properly connected

    Make sure your iOS device is connected using a working USB cable. Try unplugging and reconnecting it.

  2. Try a different USB port or adapter

    Some USB-C hubs or adapters may cause audio instability.

  3. Streaming depends on your computer’s CPU performance

    If your computer is running heavy apps, audio may stutter slightly.

Try this

  1. Close unnecessary programs

  2. Restart your computer to stabilise performance

    Restarting can help reset audio routing and improve streaming smoothness.

I can’t hear any audio

Wi-Fi mode

  1. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network

    Your computer and iOS device must be connected to the same network.

  2. Increase the volume on your device

    Wallas Audio has a fixed system level, but device volume is adjustable.

  3. Confirm your hearing device is properly connected

    If your hearing device is disconnected, you may not hear the streamed audio.

USB mode

  1. Confirm the USB cable is properly connected

    Make sure your device is connected to your computer using a working USB cable. Try unplugging and reconnecting it.

  2. Try a different USB port or adapter

    Some USB-C hubs or adapters may not transfer audio correctly. Test another port if needed.

  3. Increase the volume on your device

    Wallas Audio has a fixed system level, but device volume is adjustable.

  4. Confirm your hearing device is properly connected

    If your hearing device is disconnected, you may not hear the streamed audio.

Setup & everyday use

Getting it running, and living with it

How do I install Wallas Audio?

Wallas Audio is the virtual audio driver required for streaming.

Install

  1. Install Wallas Audio

    You can get it from the download page.

  2. Restart your computer so the virtual audio device appears

  3. Open your computer sound settings

  4. Select Wallas Audio as your input and output device

    In your sound settings, set Wallas Audio for both input and output so your computer’s sound is routed into Wallas’ Stream.

After your session, don’t forget to switch back to your normal speakers so you can hear your computer audio again.

How do I switch between Connected Mode and Meeting Mode?

To toggle modes

  1. Double-press the main circle button inside the app

    Meeting Mode helps prevent the stream from disconnecting during quiet moments. When enabled (green), audio automatically pauses when silence is detected.
