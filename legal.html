Privacy Policy

Overview

  • We do not require user accounts.
  • We do not use cloud storage.
  • We do not collect personal data as part of normal operation.
  • We do not send personal data to external servers.

All processing happens locally and only during active use. Wallas’ Stream does not perform background recording or continuous listening when the App is not actively running.

Who we are

Wallas’ Stream is an independent application operated by Renata A. Ponce P.

Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Contacts
  • Location data
  • Audio recordings
  • Voice or biometric data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • User tracking or identity-linked analytics

Wallas Audio (virtual audio device)

  • Routes system audio in real time
  • Does not record, store, or analyze audio
  • Does not transmit data externally
  • Runs only when explicitly selected by the user

Audio handling

Desktop app

  • Audio is transmitted live and temporarily
  • Audio is never stored
  • The user manually selects the audio output device
  • The App does not automatically change system audio outputs
  • No audio persists after the session ends

Transmission can occur via

  • Local network (Wi-Fi)
  • Direct wired connection (USB cable)

iOS app

  • Receives audio only
  • Plays audio in real time
  • Never records or stores audio

Permissions used

Desktop system may request

  • Audio / Microphone access Required by the system for real-time audio routing and playback. The system may label this permission as “Microphone” even when no audio is recorded or stored.
  • Local Network access Used to connect directly to your computer on the same network.

iOS system may request

  • Camera Used only to scan QR codes to establish a connection.
  • Bluetooth Used only to connect to user-approved external audio or hearing devices.
  • Local Network Used for direct audio transmission from your computer.
  • Notifications (optional) Used only to alert you when the connection is disconnected due to prolonged silence or inactivity. You can use the App without enabling notifications.

Local data stored on your device

  • First use date
  • Listening time today
  • Listening time this week
  • Total listening time

This data never leaves your device, is not linked to your identity, and is not shared with Wallas’ Stream or any third party.

Notifications

  • Do not contain personal data
  • Are not used for tracking, marketing, or advertising

Widgets and system indicators

  • Display only local connection status and listening time
  • Use data stored locally on the device
  • Do not transmit any data to external servers
  • Do not collect personal or identifying information

Third-party services

  • Uses no advertising SDKs
  • Uses no third-party analytics
  • Uses no tracking frameworks

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, no child data is stored.

Data security

Since no personal data is transmitted or stored externally, exposure risk is minimal. Connections are temporary, local, and initiated by the user.

By using Wallas’ Stream, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.
