Before you send

What to include

The more of this you can tell us, the faster we can help.

  • Topic Early access, installation, a streaming issue, or a bug report.
  • Your setup Mac model and macOS version, iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Your hearing device Brand and model of your sound processor or hearing aid.
  • How you connected Wi-Fi or cable, and which one you tried first.
  • What happened What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots (optional) A screenshot or a short screen recording helps a lot.

Wallas’ Stream is an independent tool and not a medical device. For anything about your processor’s clinical settings or programming, please speak to your audiologist or clinic.
