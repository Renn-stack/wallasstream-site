Setup

Five steps, in order

From installing on your Mac to listening on your hearing device.

  1. 1

    Download Wallas’ Stream for Mac

    The main app that streams your Mac’s audio. Compatible with Intel and Apple Silicon.

    Download the Mac app

    Wallas Stream 1.0.0 · .dmg · macOS

  2. 2

    Download Wallas Audio

    The virtual audio driver that routes your Mac’s sound into Wallas’ Stream. Required for audio to reach your device.

    Download Wallas Audio

    Wallas Audio 1.0.0 (English) · .dmg · macOS

  3. 3

    Restart your Mac

    Restart so the virtual audio device appears. Then, in System Settings, choose Wallas Virtual Audio as your Mac’s sound output.

  4. 4

    Download Wallas’ Stream for iPhone

    The companion app for your phone. It receives the audio and sends it to your hearing device.

  5. 5

    Start streaming: Wi-Fi or cable

    On your iPhone, open Wallas’ Stream and choose how you want to connect.

    Wi-Fi mode

    1. Open Wallas’ Stream for Mac
    2. Choose Wi-Fi mode
    3. Put both devices on the same 5 GHz Wi-Fi network
    4. Scan the QR code shown in the Mac app

    USB mode

    1. Open Wallas’ Stream for Mac
    2. Select USB mode
    3. Connect your iPhone to your Mac with a Lightning or USB-C cable

Both the Mac app and Wallas Audio are required for streaming to work.
