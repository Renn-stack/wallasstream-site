Before you start

Questions people ask first

Will this affect my hearing device settings?

No. Wallas’ Stream does not change your processor settings or clinical programming in any way.

It streams audio from your computer to your device, which then delivers sound through your existing hearing setup, just like any other audio app.

  • No changes to MAPs or audiology settings
  • No firmware updates
  • No medical adjustments
Which hearing devices does Wallas’ Stream work with?

Wallas’ Stream has been tested with Cochlear™ Nucleus™ 7 and Nucleus™ 8 processors connected through a compatible iOS device.

It works as a private audio streaming companion, using your existing hearing setup. Compatibility may vary depending on your processor model and device audio settings.

Your first 7 days include Pro, so you can try it with your own setup.

Does Wallas’ Stream record audio?

No. Audio is streamed live for listening and is not stored or recorded by the system.

Is this a medical device?

No. Wallas’ Stream is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

Is Windows support available?

Wallas’ Stream is currently available for macOS and iOS devices. We’re actively working on expanding support to other platforms, including Android and Windows.
